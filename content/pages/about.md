I am a geomatician based in Munich. For more than five years I have processed, analysed and automated geodata, most recently
for three years on the pipelines that integrate and update map data from over 40 countries for Apple Maps.

My background mixes geography, geomatics and journalism, which shows in how I like to work: build the tool, then explain clearly
how it works and why. That is also why this portfolio is written as case studies rather than as a gallery.

Since February 2026 I have been deepening my software skills through projects of my own, in Python, QGIS and web technologies.
Two of them are on this site.

## Working with AI tools

I use AI coding assistants in my own projects, and I say so openly. My part is the problem, the data, the design decisions and the testing
against real files and real maps. The assistants speed up the typing. Each case study says what I decided and what I learned.
